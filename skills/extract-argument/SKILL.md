# Extract Argument

Use when Mike supplies notes, drafts, transcripts, slides, code comments,
citations, or other artifacts and needs the underlying argument made explicit.

If available, read `../../references/writing-skill-common.md` for source
hierarchy and verification goals.

## Mode

AUDIT or PRE-DRAFT. Do not rewrite prose unless explicitly asked.

## Procedure

1. Identify the bounded unit or artifact set.
2. Extract the central claim.
3. Extract subclaims and map them to source material.
4. Separate evidence from assumptions.
5. Preserve uncertainty and caveats.
6. Identify implications without converting them into claims.
7. Infer audience and register only when source material supports it.
8. Mark gaps rather than filling them.

## Output

Use this structure:

- Central claim
- Subclaims
- Evidence
- Assumptions
- Uncertainty and caveats
- Implications
- Audience
- Intended register
- Constraints
- Gaps / author decisions

For high-stakes work, include a claim map:

| Claim ID | Claim | Source | Evidence | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Verification

- Every major claim maps to supplied material or current conversation.
- Uncertainty is preserved.
- Assumptions are separated from evidence.
- No new interpretation is smuggled in as extraction.
- Missing evidence is visible.
