# /harden-claim

Re-research and harden a load-bearing, contestable claim for:

`$ARGUMENTS`

Read these files if available:

- `references/writing-skill-common.md`
- `skills/harden-claim/SKILL.md`
- `references/independent-review-prompts.md` for fetch-verify and review patterns

State the one sharp, testable question first — if you can't, stop and draft it.
As the lead, fan out read-only breadth-search subagents (cross model families
yourself; a subagent cannot spawn subagents), fetch-verify every identifier and
headline figure against the live source rather than recalling it, then synthesise
honestly and reframe on overreach. Surface evidence including what cuts against
the claim; the author sets claim strength. Fold verified evidence into the
ledgers. To audit prose that already exists rather than re-research a claim, use
`/claim-audit` instead.
