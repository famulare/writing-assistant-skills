(slop-scan tell-list over your own draft + re-assert ledger-hardened claim

rather than filling gaps with plausible prose. Record the fixed anchor, every
span's transport-vs-hole route, connective-tissue markers, and span provenance;
without that record the result is incomplete, not a validated transport.
