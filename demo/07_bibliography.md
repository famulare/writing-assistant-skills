# Bibliography — serotype-fuzziness demo

Fetch-verified in Phase 3 of the original run (see
`reviews/original-autonomous-run/harden-citations.md` and
`reviews/original-autonomous-run/harden-facts.md`).
The 1949 papers appeared in the **American Journal of Hygiene** (renamed the American
Journal of Epidemiology in 1965 — the source email's "AJE, 1949" is that anachronism).

1. Bodian D. **Differentiation of types of poliomyelitis viruses. I. Reinfection
   experiments in monkeys (second attacks).** Am J Hyg. 1949;49(2):200–223. PMID 18113219.
2. Morgan IM. **Differentiation of types of poliomyelitis viruses. II. …by reciprocal
   vaccination-immunity experiments.** Am J Hyg. 1949;49(2):225–233. PMID 18124443.
   — reciprocal cross-immunity; basis for the Kotter symmetric cross-protection reading.
3. Bodian D, Morgan IM, Howe HA. **Differentiation of types of poliomyelitis viruses.
   III. The grouping of fourteen strains into three basic immunological types.** Am J
   Hyg. 1949;49(2):234–245. PMID 18113220.
   — the three-serotype scheme; the 14-strain grouping; the Per grouping.

## Context references (verified, not primary evidence)
- Burnet FM, Macnamara J (1931) — earliest demonstration of poliovirus antigenic types
  (two). Basis for softening "first to establish three types."
- NFIP Committee on Typing (~1951, Am J Hyg vol. 54) — confirmed exactly three types.
- MEF-1 strain provenance (Middle East Forces, 1942, Cairo; type 2) — Smithsonian
  object record; corroborated by primary correspondence.

## Not independently verifiable (primary-table / author-sourced)
- "Per" and "Kotter" strain names and provenance — present only in the primary 1949
  tables as the author read them; absent from indexed secondary literature. Cited to the
  primary source (refs 2–3); no secondary citation fabricated.
