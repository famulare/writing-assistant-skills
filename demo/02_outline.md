## Per-section contracts

### §1 The partition we trust
- **Purpose:** state the thesis — the three-serotype certainty is partly manufactured.
- **Must-claim:** serotypes are treated as fixed; that fixity is partly a standardization artifact.
- **Must-not-claim:** that serotypes aren't real / are interchangeable.
- **Source-hooks:** "Everything is less black and white than the dogma today."
- **Beats:** the received view → the crack in it → thesis.

### §2 What 1949 already knew
- **Purpose:** establish that fuzziness is old news, not a new worry.
- **Must-claim:** pre-standardization workers measured cross-immunity across many monkeys; significant cross-immunity is counter to later dogma.
- **Must-not-claim:** that the dogma was invented dishonestly; that cross-immunity means no types. **Do not disclose §4's payoff** — the cell-culture/reference-strain standardization *mechanism* and the 4-of-14 count are §4's; §2 only names the standardization horizon, it doesn't explain it. *(scaffold-audit fix, log #5)*
- **Source-hooks:** "figuring out what serotypes were by measuring cross-immunity with lots of monkeys"; the "two to four generations" lineage of inherited dogma.
- **Beats:** the surprise → why we're surprised (generational distance from the founders) → point forward to the standardization horizon (named, not explained).

### §3 The scheme and its exceptions
- **Purpose:** give the concrete historical evidence.
- **Must-claim:** Bodian 1949 fixes 3 serotypes + the challenge pattern; **Per** is grouped type 1 but cross-protected by type 2; **Kover** is symmetric (Bodian + Morgan).
- **Must-not-claim:** any specific table number not verified; that the exceptions are artifact-free (author keeps the "could be noise, but…" hedge).
- **Source-hooks:** the Per provenance (3-yr-old, WV 1940, Sabin), the "2/6 when expectation is 6/6 is weird" reproducibility aside.
- **Beats:** the normal pattern → Per breaks it → Kover confirms the phenomenon → the reproducibility caveat.

### §4 How fuzziness was defined away
- **Purpose:** mechanism of the artifact.
- **Must-claim:** early-1950s cell culture + reference-strain standardization operationally removed the ambiguity; most isolates dropped out (only 4 of 14 survived to sequencing).
- **Must-not-claim:** the count as fact if unverified (mark it); that standardization was wrong (it was fit for the vaccine purpose).
- **Source-hooks:** "If you're vaccinating against three types, none of this nuance matters. But in the canonizing of stylized facts, we lost the common knowledge that the story is fuzzy."
- **Beats:** what standardization did → why it was reasonable → what was lost.

### §5 An evolutionary reading (speculation, labeled)
- **Purpose:** offer the mechanism for when fuzziness matters.
- **Must-claim:** heterogeneous cross-serotype immunity can select for/permit off-type persistence; trivalent vaccination synchronized immunity and suppressed it.
- **Must-not-claim:** that this is established; keep "let's assume / could / in principle."
- **Source-hooks:** seasonal serotype waves; "this year's type 1 outbreak would do better if it was a little more type 2 like"; Per as a candidate.
- **Beats:** the fitness assumption → pre-vaccine dynamics → the trivalent collapse.

### §6 Why it is live again
- **Purpose:** the forecast and the landing.
- **Must-claim:** modern WPV = strong purifying selection + tiny diversity + VP1-only typing → off-type invisible; OPV2 cessation re-heterogenizes immunity; "mostly type 1 with a bit of type 2" should not be surprising; expect to start noticing between-type viruses.
- **Must-not-claim:** that such viruses have been confirmed; keep it a calibrated expectation.
- **Source-hooks:** "we drop the type 2 vaccine, and population immunity across serotypes becomes much more heterogeneous than ever before … we wait a few years and start to notice viruses that are between serotype…."
- **Beats:** why we don't see it now → what changed → what to expect → landing.
