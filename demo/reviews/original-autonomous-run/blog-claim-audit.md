## Findings

### Finding 1 — thesis quantifier intensified: "a good chunk" (LOW)
- **Span:** "a good chunk of that hardness is something we manufactured in the 1950s" (¶1).
- **Issue:** Ledger C1 is calibrated to **"partly"**, and report §1 renders it "some of its
  hardness is manufactured." "A good chunk" reads as a larger, more quantified share than
  "some/partly." A mild upward nudge of the load-bearing thesis quantifier.
- **Severity:** LOW — still hedged, not promoted to "most/mostly"; casual register tolerates
  looser quantifiers. But it is the one place the blog is slightly firmer than the ledger.
- **Fix (optional):** "some of that hardness" or "a fair bit of that hardness" if the author
  wants to hold C1 exactly at "partly."

### Finding 2 — dropped temporal qualifier: "barely drifts antigenically" (LOW)
- **Span:** "Modern wild poliovirus is under such strong purifying selection that it barely
  drifts antigenically" (§"why now").
- **Issue:** Source and report both qualify this temporally — "never drift very far
  antigenically **for very long**" / "do not drift far antigenically **for long**." The blog
  drops the "for long." "Barely drifts" reads as a stronger, more static claim than
  "doesn't drift far for long."
- **Severity:** LOW — nuance loss, not a sign/direction error; consistent with author's field
  view either way.
- **Fix (optional):** "barely drifts antigenically for long."

### Finding 3 — "only four survived to be sequenced" stated flat (LOW / no action)
- **Span:** "Of the fourteen strains in Bodian's grouping, only four survived to be sequenced
  decades later" (§"how we lost the plot").
- **Issue:** Ledger C10 is AUTHOR-SOURCED with limited external verification (14→3 verified;
  the exact surviving set not independently confirmed). The blog states it as bare fact.
  **However** the report §4 also states it inline without citation, so the blog does not
  promote strength relative to its parent — and it omits the named set (Brunhilde/Lansing/
  MEF-1/Leon), which reduces rather than inflates the specific assertion.
- **Severity:** LOW / no action for this artifact — strength matches the report. Noted only so
  the author knows C10's evidence status carries through unchanged.
