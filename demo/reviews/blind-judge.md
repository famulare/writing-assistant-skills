# Independent fresh-context judge

Blind quality judgment of two finished pieces (`05_scientific-report.md`,
`08_blog-post.md`) transported from one source email
(`raw_source_serotype-fuzziness.md`) into two registers, scored against the
scientific and casual voice cards. I judged; I did not rewrite. Scores are 1–5.

## The six-beat arc (reference)

1. Received partition (three serotypes treated as a hard fact)
2. 1949 they already knew serotypes were fuzzy
3. Bodian/Morgan evidence, with Per & Kover as the misbehaving strains
4. Early-1950s standardization defined the fuzziness away
5. Evolutionary reading (heterogeneous immunity selects off-type drift)
6. OPV2 cessation makes it live again

## Scientific report (`05_scientific-report.md`)

| Axis | Score | Justification |
|---|---|---|
| Arc completeness | 5 | All six beats present, one per section (§1 partition → §6 live again); no beat dropped, and §3 carries both Per and Kover. |
| Register separation | 5 | Formal-scientific and genuinely so — footnoted journal citations, precise hedging ("conditional," "I can't rule out") — without going dead or institutional; it still lands "is weird." |

- **Weakest:** it removes the personal/emotional seam (the West Virginia girl beat is reduced to a clause), so at moments it reads like a competent stranger's formalization rather than Mike's.

---

## Which transport succeeded better

The **blog** is the more complete success. It is arc-complete, cleanly separated in
register, *and* the most faithful to the source's actual voice — because the source
is itself an excited, emotionally-exposed email, the casual register is its natural
home, and the blog keeps the one beat (the West Virginia girl) that is the strongest
authorial fingerprint in the whole email. Its only cost is a small edge-of-frame
darling ("seventy years").

The scientific report is arguably the harder lift — turning a giddy email into
defensible, citation-hardened prose while holding every hedge and claim strength in
place — and it largely nails it. But that transport necessarily sheds the source's
emotional register, which is a genuine faithfulness cost, not just a stylistic one.
So: the scientific version is the more impressive engineering feat; the blog is the
better-succeeded transport on the stated axes.

Both are genuinely one argument in two voices, not two arguments — the substrate
fidelity holds across the board, and the register separation is real, not cosmetic.
