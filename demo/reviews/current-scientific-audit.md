## What is working

- Per and Kotter remain attributed to the primary tables; sampling variation and laboratory error remain local to the Kotter inference; “Two of six … is weird” preserves the author's calibration rather than upgrading it to a finding.
- The evolutionary account is explicitly introduced as conjecture and retains its assumptions. The historical evidence, proposed immunity mechanism, and forecast are not presented as one established result.
- “In canonizing those stylized facts, the field lost the common knowledge that the story was fuzzy” and the final “mostly type 1 with a little bit of type 2” retain useful compression and a direct landing. They should not be removed as slop.
- Headings are descriptive rather than ornamental. No generic implications paragraph, corporate diction, fake symmetry, or citation-less literature wave remains.
