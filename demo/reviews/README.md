# Demo review record

`original-autonomous-run/` preserves the independent reviews produced during the
first autonomous demo. Those reports evaluated the pre-correction versions of the
scientific report and blog post. They are retained as workflow evidence, including
evidence of what the original audit stack missed; they do not validate the current
artifacts.

`current-scientific-audit.md` and `current-blog-audit.md` evaluate the corrected
parallel transports. They surface residual author decisions rather than treating
assistant adjudication as human approval.

`voice-costume-blog-audit.md` and `voice-costume-scientific-audit.md` are the
first tests of the whole-artifact `voice-costume-audit`. Each preserves a hashed
artifact-only blind profile followed by an independent authority reveal. The
blog receives **No material costume**; the scientific report receives
**Localized costume pressure** around the scope and duration of the speaker's
historical-literature expertise.
