## Verdict

**No material costume** — **high confidence**.

The dominant speaker mechanisms identified blind are directly present in the primary email, often verbatim. The outline governs order and the review apparatus governs provenance, but neither supplies a substitute personality. The strongest discriminator that could change this verdict would be an author judgment that publishing near-verbatim private-email intimacy (“legit tearing up,” “Lord knows,” “a hoot”) is not acceptable in this public context despite the dossier's general permission for such texture; no accepted/rejected prose examples for this artifact were supplied.
