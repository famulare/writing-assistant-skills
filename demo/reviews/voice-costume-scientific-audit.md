### Verdict

**Localized costume pressure** — **moderate confidence**.

The artifact’s mechanisms, technical claims, first-person accountability, uncertainty, cross-scale reasoning, and most of its friction are strongly source-earned or contract-required. The localized pressure is concentrated in the performed historical authority: removing the direct admission that this literature was new to the author lets the formalized note imply longer-standing historical-serotyping expertise than the supplied primary voice supports. A secondary, weaker pressure comes from assistant-authored formal connective language, but the approved opener and formal-note contract substantially govern that register shift.

**Strongest discriminator that could change the verdict:** an explicit author decision on whether omitting “I have spent zero time with [this literature] prior to yesterday” is an intentional and accurate formal-register choice. Approval would likely move the verdict to **No material costume**; a requirement to preserve that self-location would confirm the present finding and raise confidence.

- The generational and standardization account of how ambiguity left routine view.
- The explicit conjecture/assumption boundary around the evolutionary mechanism.
- Population-immunity heterogeneity as the bridge from historical evidence to vaccine-era dynamics.
